## Investigated but not integrated

- **Atmospheric dust opacity / storms (MCS)** — MCS retrievals are along-track vertical profiles (DDR/EDR). No gridded, time-resolved product could be retrieved and validated in this build; the 2018 storm is covered by the user-supplied papers only. https://pdsatmospheres.nmsu.edu/data_and_services/atmospheres_data/MARS/aerosols.html
- **SHARAD radargrams** — Radargrams require per-track interpretation; integrated indirectly through the SWIM composite which uses SHARAD surface power and dielectric estimates. https://sharad.psi.edu/
- **CRISM targeted observations / MTRDR** — Footprint-level products are tens of GB; not retrievable here. TES global mineral abundances are used instead and labelled with their detection limits. https://pds-geosciences.wustl.edu/missions/mro/crism.htm
- **Gridded surface temperature** — No gridded temperature product integrated. Thermal inertia is a material property, not temperature. Survival module uses published RAD/REMS ranges. 
- **RAD RDR time series** — PDS PPI RDR tables not retrieved; the radiation model is anchored to published RAD results instead. https://search-pdsppi.igpp.ucla.edu/ditdos/readme?id=pds://PPI/MSL-M-RAD-3-RDR-V1.0
- **Soil engineering properties (cohesion, bearing capacity)** — No global measured dataset exists. Values in reports are labelled as literature ranges from rover sites.
